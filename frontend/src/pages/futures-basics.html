What are futures?

A futures contract lets you trade the price movement of an asset without owning the underlying coin or commodity directly. TraidiumX offers perpetual futures — contracts with no expiry date, so you can hold a position for minutes, days, or months as long as your margin supports it.

Markets include major cryptocurrencies such as BTC, ETH, SOL, high-volatility meme coins, precious metals like Gold (XAU) and Silver, plus selected global energy and stock indexes.

Long and short

Unlike traditional spot markets where you only make money when prices go up, futures give you the power to generate returns in both bull and bear market trends.

↗ Long (Buy) Bullish Bias

Profit when price rises

Open a Long position when you anticipate the market will climb. If the asset appreciates, your trade gains value.

Result: +$3,000 profit
↘ Short (Sell) Bearish Bias

Leverage and margin

Margin is the collateral amount allocated from your wallet to support an open position. Leverage acts as a multiplier on your purchasing power, allowing you to open a position much larger than your allocated margin.

For instance, with 100 USDT margin at 10x leverage, you control a 1,000 USDT market position.

Margin Leverage Position Size 1% Price Move
100 USDT 1x (Spot equivalent) 100 USDT ±1 USDT (±1%)
100 USDT 10x 1,000 USDT ±10 USDT (±10%)
100 USDT 50x 5,000 USDT ±50 USDT (±50%)
Risk Notice: Higher leverage increases capital efficiency, but also amplifies loss rates. A 2% adverse market move at 50x leverage leads to liquidation if stop losses are not used.

Cross vs Isolated

TraidiumX allows you to choose how margin is segregated for each trade:

Isolated Margin

Collateral is strictly restricted to this single trade. If the trade is liquidated, only the margin locked for this specific position is lost.

✓ Recommended for new traders and high-risk setups.

Cross Margin

Your entire trading wallet balance is shared across all active open positions to prevent premature liquidation during volatile swings.

✓ Ideal for hedging and multi-asset position management.

Take profit & stop loss

Professional risk management is essential. TraidiumX lets you configure automated Take Profit (TP) and Stop Loss (SL) orders directly before opening or at any time during an active trade.

Take Profit (TP)
Lock in gains automatically

Closes the trade once your profit target is reached, preventing gains from reversing.

Stop Loss (SL)
Cap maximum loss

Exits the trade automatically if price moves against you, safeguarding your remaining capital.

Pro Tip: You can also use Trailing Stops to track market momentum and automatically lock in profits as trends progress.

Liquidation & how to prevent it

Liquidation occurs when your position's loss approaches the maintenance margin threshold. When the Mark Price hits your designated Liquidation Price, the exchange automatically closes your position to prevent account balance deficit.

3 Essential Rules to avoid liquidation:

  • 1 Always set a Stop Loss before entering high-leverage trades.
  • 2 Use lower leverage (5x to 10x) on volatile crypto pairs to increase price buffer.
  • 3 Use Isolated Margin so one trade cannot affect your overall wallet balance.
