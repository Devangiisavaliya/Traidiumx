How to deposit funds?

100% Free & Instant Credit • 0% Deposit Fees

At TraidiumX, we provide institutional-grade security and ultra-fast deposit channels. You can deposit funds using Cryptocurrency (USDT, BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB) or Fiat Money (INR via instant UPI, Net Banking, and Bank Transfer).

All deposits are credited with 0% processing fee directly to your TraidiumX Main Wallet. From there, you can instantly allocate funds to your Futures, Options, MT5, or Sub-Accounts with zero transfer fees.

Quick Verification Tip: For higher limits and seamless INR withdrawals, please complete your basic KYC identity verification in Profile → Verification . It only takes under 2 minutes.

Select a Deposit Method:

₿

Deposit Crypto (USDT, BTC, ETH, SOL)

TRC20, BEP20, ERC20, Polygon & Solana Networks

1

Go to your TraidiumX Wallet and click the Deposit button.

2

Select Crypto Deposit and choose your coin (e.g., USDT, BTC, ETH).

3

Select your preferred network (e.g., TRC20, BEP20, ERC20). Make sure the network matches your sender wallet.

4

Copy your personal deposit wallet address or scan the dynamic QR code with Binance, Trust Wallet, MetaMask, or any exchange.

5

Confirm the withdrawal from your external wallet. Your TraidiumX Main Wallet will be automatically credited within 1-3 network confirmations.

Go to Deposit Crypto →
₹

Deposit Money (INR via UPI & Bank Transfer)

GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, IMPS & Net Banking

1

Go to TraidiumX Wallet and click Deposit → Deposit Money (INR).

2

Enter the amount in INR (e.g. ₹1,000 to ₹5,000,000). The live USD conversion rate is calculated with 0% conversion markup.

3

Choose Instant UPI QR or Bank IMPS / RTGS. Open Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm and complete the payment.

4

Enter the 12-digit UTR / Transaction Reference Number in the verification box and click Submit.

5

Our automated reconciliation engine verifies the UTR and credits your wallet in 30-60 seconds.

Go to Deposit INR →
⇄

Internal Transfer to Trading Accounts

Instant 0-Fee transfer between Main Wallet, Futures, MT5 & Sub-Accounts

1

Click on the Transfer button on your wallet overview.

2

Select the source account (e.g. Main Wallet) and the destination account (e.g. Futures Trading Account or MetaTrader 5).

3

Enter the amount and confirm. Transfers are processed in 0 seconds with zero charges.

Important Security Guidelines

  • Always double check the blockchain network (e.g., TRC20 vs ERC20) before transferring crypto.
  • For INR deposits, always paste the exact 12-digit UTR provided by your bank app.
  • TraidiumX staff will never ask for your account password, 2FA codes, or private keys.
