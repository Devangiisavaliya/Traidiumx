Why KYC-AML Matters

Protecting users and preventing misuse.

KYC and AML controls help identify users, monitor suspicious transactions and reduce financial crime risk.

Identity Verification

Verify user identity before deposits, withdrawals, trading and campaign participation.

AML Monitoring

Detect suspicious wallet, trading, withdrawal and referral activity.

Compliance Review

Support legal, regulatory, tax and reporting responsibilities where applicable.

Fraud Prevention

Reduce fake accounts, duplicate accounts, identity misuse and abuse of platform rewards.
