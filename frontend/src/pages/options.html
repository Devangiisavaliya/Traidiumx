Options Guide

Quick reference for trading options on TraidiumX

Calls & Puts

A Call gives you the right to buy the underlying at the strike price. A Put gives you the right to sell. You pay premium upfront; profit depends on how spot moves before expiry.

Strike & Expiry

Each contract has a strike (exercise price) and expiry date. Pick the expiry tab first, then choose a strike near spot for ATM options or further away for OTM / ITM plays.

ITM / ATM / OTM

In-the-money (ITM) options have intrinsic value. At-the-money (ATM) strikes sit closest to spot. Out-of-the-money (OTM) options are cheaper but need a larger move to profit.

Reading the chain

Bid / Ask show where you can sell or buy. Mark is the fair value used for margin. IV (implied volatility) reflects how expensive options are. OI shows open interest at each strike.

Greeks

Delta measures price sensitivity to spot. Toggle Greeks on the chain to view Δ and IV per strike. Higher IV usually means higher premium and more time decay risk.

Before you trade

Options can expire worthless. Use limit orders when possible, size positions within your wallet balance, and confirm the correct contract (BTC/ETH, expiry, Call/Put) before placing.

Δ Delta (Price Sensitivity)

Measures how much the option premium changes for every $1.00 move in the underlying crypto price. Delta 0.50 means a $100 price rise increases premium by $50.

Γ Gamma (Delta Speed)

Measures the rate of change of Delta. High Gamma means Delta shifts rapidly as market price moves closer to ATM strike.

Θ Theta (Daily Time Decay)

Measures daily loss of option premium due to time passing. As Expiry approaches, Theta decay accelerates (negative value).

ν Vega (Volatility Impact)

Measures how much premium changes for every 1% change in Implied Volatility (IV%).
