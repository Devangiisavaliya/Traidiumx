Referral Questions

Simple answers for referral and affiliate users.

How does referral earning work?

You share your backend-generated referral link and earn rewards when referred users complete eligible trading activity. Final reward rules are controlled by backend and company policy.

Can I generate a new referral code?

Referral codes are generated securely from backend and assigned to authenticated users. Users can copy and share their assigned code/link.

Is affiliate approval automatic?

No. Affiliate applications are stored in backend for company operations review. This project does not add a separate management panel.
