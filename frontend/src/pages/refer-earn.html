How does referral earning work?

Simply copy and share your unique referral link with your friends. Once they sign up and start trading on TraidiumX, you will earn a percentage of their trading fees as a commission, paid directly to your wallet in real-time.

Can I generate a new referral code?

Your primary referral code is automatically generated when you create your account. You can view, copy, and share this code directly from your Referral Dashboard. If you need custom codes for marketing campaigns, you can contact our affiliate support.

Is there a limit to how many friends I can invite?

No, there is absolutely no limit! You can invite as many friends as you like and earn continuous commission on all their trades.
