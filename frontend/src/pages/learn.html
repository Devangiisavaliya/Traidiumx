Trading Dictionary

A to Z Crypto Glossary

Essential trading terminology explained in crisp, clear language.

Bullish

A market condition or trader sentiment expecting prices to trend upward.

Bearish

A downward market condition characterized by heavy selling pressure and falling asset prices.

Leverage

Using borrowed capital to amplify trading position sizes (e.g. 10x, 50x, 100x), magnifying both gains and risks.

Liquidation

The automatic closure of a leveraged position by the exchange when margin collateral is depleted.

Long Position

Buying an asset or contract with the expectation that its market value will rise over time.

Short Position

Selling an asset or derivative contract to profit from an anticipated downward price movement.

Stop-Loss (SL)

A predetermined order placed to automatically exit an open trade and prevent excessive financial loss.

Take-Profit (TP)

A preset limit order designed to automatically close a position once a specified profit target is reached.

Spread

The numerical price difference between the highest buy price (Bid) and the lowest sell price (Ask) in the order book.

Slippage

The variance between the expected price of a trade and the actual executed price during volatile market swings.

P&L (Profit & Loss)

The total net financial gain or loss realized or unrealized on open and closed trading positions.

HODL

A long-term buy-and-hold cryptocurrency investment strategy regardless of short-term volatility.

What is the minimum amount required to start trading?

You can start Futures & Options trading with as little as $5.00 USDT or ₹100.00 INR. TraidiumX does not enforce any mandatory large initial balance requirements.

How does 100x leverage work on TraidiumX Futures?

With 100x leverage, a $10 margin allows you to control a $1,000 position. A 1% market move in your favor results in a 100% profit ($10 profit). However, if the market moves 1% against your entry price, your position will be liquidated. We always advise setting Stop-Loss orders on high leverage positions.

How long does INR Bank Withdrawal take?

INR Bank withdrawals are processed directly to your registered bank account via IMPS/NEFT. Once reviewed and approved by our automated security system, payouts are typically credited within 15 to 45 minutes.
