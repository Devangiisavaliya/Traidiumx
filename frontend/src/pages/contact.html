Contact FAQ

Quick answers before creating a support ticket.

Is this ticket saved in database?

Yes. All submitted tickets are saved live in MongoDB Atlas database, linked to your user profile, and can be viewed or resolved by admins.

Can users upload documents for KYC support?

Document upload is planned for a future update. Currently, KYC tickets are routed directly to the compliance admin desk for immediate review.

How will real support tracking work?

Real support tracking is fully active. You can monitor your tickets and their statuses (Open / In Progress / Resolved) directly from the history table above.
