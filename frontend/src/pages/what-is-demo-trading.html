Supported Markets & Assets

Demo trading is fully integrated into TraidiumX Futures. You can trade all major perpetual contracts including BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, plus Metals like Gold (XAUUSD) with full up to 100x leverage.

How to start in 4 easy steps:

1

Open the Futures Trading Terminal

Click Futures in the top navigation bar or go directly to the terminal.

2

Select the Demo Account

Open the account dropdown selector in the trade sidebar and choose #DEMO. Your available balance will switch to $100,000 USD virtual funds.

3

Configure and place your trade

Select your trading pair, set leverage (1x to 100x), enter trade amount, configure optional TP/SL, and click Buy / Long or Sell / Short.

4

Track & manage positions

Monitor your live unrealized PnL, edit TP/SL triggers, or close positions right from the active positions panel underneath the live chart.

Manage & Reset Demo Balance

Your demo balance starts with $100,000 USD. If your balance runs out or you want to practice with a specific amount, simply go to Futures, ensure #DEMO is selected, click Deposit (+), and choose Set Demo Balance. You can reset or top up any amount up to 10,000,000 USD anytime.

Switching to Live Trading

When you are ready to trade with real funds, deposit to your TraidiumX Main Wallet, transfer funds to your Futures trading account, and switch the account selector from #DEMO to your live trading account. Demo and live balances are always 100% separate.
